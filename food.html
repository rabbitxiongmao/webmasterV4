Food & Basic Needs Resources

State College Food Bank

Supplies food assistance to individuals and families in need through direct distribution and partnerships with local agencies.

The Lion’s Pantry

A food pantry that supports Penn State students and community members by providing access to nutritious food and basic necessities.

St. Andrew’s Episcopal Church Community Café

Serves free, nutritious meals in a welcoming environment while fostering community connection and support.

Faith Centre Food Bank

Provides food assistance, community meals, and outreach programs to help individuals and families facing food insecurity.

Meals on Wheels

Delivers healthy meals to seniors and individuals with disabilities, helping them maintain independence and proper nutrition at home.
