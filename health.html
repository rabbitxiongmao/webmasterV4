Health & Wellness Resources

Mount Nittany Medical Center

A full-service medical center providing emergency care, specialized treatment, and outpatient services to support the physical health and wellness of community members.

Centre Helps

A confidential mental health crisis center offering 24/7 support, counseling, suicide prevention services, and access to the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Center for Community Resources

Provides counseling, advocacy, and support services for individuals and families affected by trauma, abuse, or crisis situations.

Centre Volunteers in Medicine

Offers free primary medical care, dental services, and health education to uninsured and underinsured residents in Centre County.
