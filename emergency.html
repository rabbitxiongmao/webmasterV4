Emergency & Crisis Resources

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

A national 24/7 hotline providing free and confidential emotional support for individuals experiencing mental health crises or emotional distress.

Penn State Police

Provides emergency response, law enforcement services, and crime prevention to help maintain campus and community safety.

Centre Safe

Offers emergency shelter, counseling, legal advocacy, and support services for individuals affected by domestic violence and sexual assault.

Housing Transitions

Supports individuals and families experiencing homelessness through housing assistance, education programs, and crisis intervention services.
